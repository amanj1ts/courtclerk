1. Snapshot

~3.6 Qs per shift (81 Qs over 22 shifts); average 66% correct, so this is the easiest GS topic. Exam style: one-line "who / which Act / which year / what happened" questions, with the movement-era Gandhi block the safest. The hard misses were early-company, Act and obscure-reformer questions (12–38%).

  1. Lock in Gandhi 1917–1942 first (Champaran→Quit India): almost every such question scored 80%+.
  2. Learn each British Act as "one Act = one key change".
  3. Learn European companies by (year, first factory, HQ).
  4. Learn INC sessions 1885–1907 and the Moderate/Extremist split.
  5. Learn reformer → organisation/book pairs.
  6. Learn Governor-General → policy pairs.
  7. Know 1857: cause, leaders, Crown takeover.
  8. Revise traps (section 6) the day before.
  9. Do the MCQs timed: 40 sec each.
  10. Guess only after eliminating two options (¼ negative).

5. Memory aids

  • Acts order: 1773 Regulating → 1784 Pitt → 1813 (monopoly ends) → 1833 (GG of India) → 1853 (open competition) → 1858 (Crown) → 1861 → 1892 → 1909 (separate electorates) → 1919 (dyarchy) → 1935 (provincial autonomy).
  • Gandhi first-timers: Champaran 1917 (first civil disobedience, indigo) → Ahmedabad 1918 (first fast) → Kheda 1918 (first non-cooperation) → Rowlatt 1919 → NCM 1920–22 → Dandi 1930 → Quit India 1942.
  • Europeans arrival: Portuguese 1498 → English 1600 → Dutch 1602 → Danes 1616 → French 1664.
  • Confusable pairs: Rash Behari Ghosh (Surat 1907 president) ≠ Rash Behari Bose (INA/Delhi conspiracy). Servants of India Society (Gokhale 1905) ≠ Servants of the People Society (Lajpat Rai 1921). Gandhi–Irwin Pact (Delhi Pact) → Second RTC. Poona Pact ≠ Communal Award. Mahalwari (Holt Mackenzie, village) vs Ryotwari (Munro, peasant) vs Zamindari/Permanent (Cornwallis 1793).

6. Trap alerts

  • "Governor-General of India" title → Charter Act 1833 (Bentinck); "Viceroy" title → 1858 (Canning).
  • Option "Charter Act 1858" / "Regulating Act 1858" do not exist: the 1858 Act is Government of India Act.
  • Charter Act 1813 ends the Company's trade monopoly (except tea and China trade); 1833 ends commercial functions.
  • Pitt's India Act 1784 set up the Board of Control (dual control).
  • Separate electorates for Muslims → 1909, not 1892 or 1919.
  • Satyagraha ≠ passive resistance: it is non-violent, truth-based resistance.
  • Quit India slogan "Do or Die" (Gandhi) ≠ "Delhi Chalo" (Bose) ≠ "Swaraj is my birthright" (Tilak).
  • Sharda Act 1929: boys 18, girls 14 (1978 amendment: girls 18, boys 21).
  • Dutch, not British, set up the first factory at Masulipatnam (1605).
