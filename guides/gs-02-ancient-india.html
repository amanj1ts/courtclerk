1. Snapshot

Weight: ~2 Qs/shift (41 Qs in 22 shifts). Avg correct 57%, so accuracy matters; 1/4 negative marking.

How it asks: one-line facts: who/which/where. Post/title (“served as…”), capital, first-to-take-a-title, inscription↔ruler, Harappan planning. Hard ones (7–14% correct) are obscure facts: skip unless you can eliminate 2 options.

  1. Master Harappan layout, Dholavira, Lothal first (most repeated, 69–92%).
  2. Learn Rig vs Later Vedic as a contrast list.
  3. Learn dynasty → capital → inscription → author.
  4. Learn “first to…” titles/coins.
  5. Kushanas: Kanishka = Shaka era + 4th Council.
  6. Gupta: Samudragupta (Allahabad), Chandragupta II (silver, Sakari).
  7. Harsha: Thaneswar→Kanauj; Pulakeshin II beat him (Aihole).
  8. South: Narasimhavarman I = Mamalla = Mahabalipuram.
  9. Eliminate options with absolutes (“exclusively”, “alone”).
  10. Mark only if you can cut to 2 options.

4. Fact tables

Skip unless time is left (UPSC extras)
  • Harappan weights in multiples of 16; brick ratio 1:2:4; inch ≈1.75 cm.
  • Harappan imports by site (lapis: Shortughai; copper: Khetri/Oman).

  • Gupta tax names (Bhaga, Bhoga, Bali, Halivakara…), land tenures.
  • Pallava tank (Eripatti), Sangam eco-zones (Thinai), Satavahana officials.
