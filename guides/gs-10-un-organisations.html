3. Concept notes

Three UN categories. (a) Principal organs: GA, SC, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ, Secretariat. (b) Funds & programmes (created by GA): UNICEF, UNDP, UNEP, UNFPA, WFP, UNHCR, UN-Habitat. (c) Specialised agencies: separate bodies linked by agreement: ILO, FAO, UNESCO, WHO, IMF, World Bank, ICAO, ITU, IFAD, UNIDO, WIPO, IMO, UNCTAD*. (*Parmar lists UNCTAD under "other"; treat as UN body.) The WTO and IAEA are related organisations, and the WTO is not a UN specialised agency.

Why veto matters. Substantive SC resolutions need 9 of 15 votes with no negative vote from any P5 (source: "a permanent member casts a negative vote" blocks). Abstention or absence does not block. P5: Britain, France, China, USA, Russia.

GA vs SC. GA: all members, two-thirds for major decisions else simple majority, decisions not binding, approves budget. SC: 15 members, 10 non-permanent for 2 years (originally 6).

Twin organisations. IMF and World Bank (Bretton Woods 1944, Washington D.C.). FAO and WFP both Rome. IMF reserve asset SDR: basket of USD, Yen, Euro, Pound, Renminbi.

WTO. Successor to GATT; established 1995 via Marrakesh Agreement 1994; HQ Geneva; GATT 23 members. Covers goods, services (GATS), IP (TRIPS) and dispute settlement. The Appellate Body has been non-functional since 2019 (US blocked appointments).

Skip unless time is left (UPSC depth) IMF quota formula (GDP 50%, openness 30%, variability 15%, reserves 5%); IMF lending facilities (SBA, EFF, FCL, RFI, RCF); WTO "boxes" (Green/Blue/Amber, de minimis); Peace Clause (Bali 2013); World Bank vote shares; GAFA tax; Basel norms.

5. Memory aids

  • Geneva gang: ILO, WHO, WIPO, WTO, ITU, UNHCR, UNCTAD, WMO – "I Walk With Wise Intelligent Humans".
  • Rome: FAO, WFP, IFAD (all food). Vienna: IAEA, UNIDO, UNODC, OPEC. Paris: UNESCO, FATF. Montreal: ICAO (+WADA). Nairobi: UNEP, UN-Habitat. New York: UN, UNICEF, UNDP, UNFPA. Washington: IMF, World Bank. London: IMO.
  • Timeline: 1919 ILO → 1944 IMF/WB → 1945 UN, FAO, UNESCO, ICJ → 1946 UNICEF → 1948 WHO (7 Apr), IMO → 1950 UNHCR → 1957 IAEA → 1967 WIPO, ASEAN → 1985 SAARC → 1995 WTO.
  • Confusable pairs: BRICS (no HQ) vs NDB (Shanghai); SCO (Beijing) vs RATS (Tashkent); AIIB (Beijing) vs ADB (Manila); UN Day 24 Oct vs Charter signed 26 Jun; ASEAN formed Bangkok / HQ Jakarta; WTO (1995) vs GATT (1947/48: sources differ).

6. Trap alerts

  • "Which is a specialised agency?" – options mix organs (GA, SC), WTO and WHO. Only WHO qualifies (14% got it).
  • Funds/programmes (UNICEF, UNDP, WFP, UNEP) are NOT specialised agencies.
  • HQ swaps: ILO Geneva not Paris; FAO/IFAD Rome not Geneva; ICAO Montreal; IAEA Vienna not Geneva; UNEP Nairobi.
  • Year swaps: WIPO 1967 (not 1979/1985); ILO 1919 under Versailles; UN Charter 1945.
  • ICJ judges: elected by GA and SC; term 9 years; seat The Hague.
  • Veto = negative vote of a P5, not abstention.
  • Parmar says BRICS "Headquarters: Shanghai" – that belongs to NDB; BRICS itself has no HQ [VERIFY].
