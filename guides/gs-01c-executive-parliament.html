3. Concept notes

🔥 Why a Money Bill is special: money belongs to the directly elected House. So it starts only in Lok Sabha, needs the President’s recommendation, the Speaker certifies it, and Rajya Sabha can only recommend (14 days). No joint sitting is needed. Defeat in LS means the government resigns.

🔥 Nominal vs real executive: President (Art 74) and Governor (Art 163) act on advice of Council of Ministers; the PM/CM is the real head. Governor has discretion in limited cases (no clear majority for CM, reserving bills, recommending President’s Rule). Advice tendered to the President cannot be inquired into by a court (Art 74(2)).

🔥 Collective responsibility: Council is responsible to Lok Sabha (Art 75) and to the Assembly in states (Art 164). A minister who disagrees with a Cabinet decision is expected to resign.

⭐ Rajya Sabha vs Legislative Council: RS is permanent (1/3 retire every 2 years). Council is also permanent but can be abolished or created (Art 169). Parliament passes it by simple majority and it is not an Art 368 amendment.

⭐ Ordinances: President Art 123, Governor Art 213, only when the House is not in session. They must be approved within 6 weeks of reassembly (max life 6 months + 6 weeks).

6. Trap alerts

  • 🔥 “1/4 nominated by the Governor” is wrong. It is 1/6 (shift Q: incorrect statement about Vidhan Parishad).
  • 🔥 “Money bill can be introduced in Rajya Sabha” is false. LS only, by a minister.
  • 🔥 Art 72 vs 161: Governor cannot pardon a death sentence or court-martial.
  • 🔥 Art 77 = business of Government of India; Art 76 = Attorney General; Art 78 = PM’s duty to inform the President; Art 74 = aid and advise.
  • 🔥 Financial committee: only PAC, Estimates, COPU. Standing committees scrutinise bills, they do not pass them.
  • 🔥 Art 167 NOT-type: CM need not submit every minister’s decision before implementation; he submits a matter only if the Governor requires.
  • ⭐ 61st = voting age 18; 52nd = anti-defection; 44th = property deleted; 86th = Art 21A; 97th = cooperatives.
  • ⭐ Delhi has 70 seats, Puducherry 33; Chandigarh and Ladakh have no assembly.
