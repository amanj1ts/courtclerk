    1. Snapshot

    Weight: about 0.6 questions per shift (14 across 22 shifts). Average 60% correct. Shift difficulty here: 6 easy, 2 moderate, 3 hard (plus 3 hard and moderate mixed, see table). Neighbours also appear inside Art & Culture and Modern History.

    How it is asked: one-line "who / which / where / when" about rulers, battles, monuments and officials. Hard questions (18-37% correct) test obscure items such as the old name of Agra Fort or the Delhi Sultanate finance post.

    1. Learn dynasty order and years first: Slave 1206, Khalji 1290, Tughlaq 1320, Sayyid 1414, Lodi 1451, Mughal 1526.
    2. Pair every ruler with ONE signature act (Iltutmish: capital to Delhi; Alauddin: price control; Tughlaq: Daulatabad).
    3. Pair every monument with its BUILDER and its CITY.
    4. Pair every battle with its YEAR and its LOSER.
    5. Learn official titles by department (Wazir = finance, Ariz = army, Mir Bakshi = Mughal military).
    6. Revenue systems: Iqta, Kharaj (Alauddin), Rai/crop-rate (Sher Shah), Zabt and Dahsala (Akbar), Mansabdari.
    7. Learn author-book pairs and traveller-country pairs.
    8. Saints: Bhakti vs Sufi, then their works.
    9. Do the 30 MCQs timed (1 minute each). Skip a question if two options remain after 40 seconds: 1/4 negative marking.
    10. Re-read the Revision card the night before.
