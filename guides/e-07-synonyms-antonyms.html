3. Context-clue and prefix/suffix strategy

  • Contrast clue (but, despite, though, in contrast): the blank opposes its neighbour. "...contrast to her colleague's verbosity" → laconic = brief.
  • Restatement clue: sentence repeats the meaning ("revealing little" → circumspect = guarded).
  • Tone clue: stung, offended → negative word (acerbic, scathing).
  • Prefixes: in-/im-/un-/dis- = not (inadvertent, impudent, unwitting); mis- = hate/wrong (misanthrope); circum- = around (circumspect, "looks around"); pro- = forward (prognostication); ex- = out/up (extol).
  • Roots: loqu/lac = speak (eloquent, laconic); verb = word (verbose); phil = love vs mis = hate; ephemer = day (lasting a day); trench = cut (trenchant); acer/caust = burn/sharp.
  • Suffixes: -ous/-ant/-ent = adjective; -ity/-ude/-ion = noun.

6. Trap alerts

  • 🔥 Antonym asked, synonym in options: candid → honest/direct/frank are all synonyms; answer = reserved. Same for penchant (liking), incredulity (skepticism), ephemeral (evanescent, fugacious), laconic (brief, concise).
  • 🔥 Two senses: "candid" = frank (also unposed photo); "subtle" = delicate/not obvious; "oriental" = eastern; "cogent" ≠ "urgent".
  • 🔥 Look-alike words: impudent/insolent (rude) vs indolent (lazy) vs indebted vs inadvertent (unintended). Insatiable ≠ satisfied. Circumspect ≠ circumstantial. Prognostication = prediction ≠ retrospection.
  • 🔥 Near-synonym tie: trenchant → incisive when both appear; scathing → harsh/biting. Pick the closest to the sentence's tone.
  • ⭐ Antonym pair-in-sentence: find the two concepts that oppose (vitality–stagnation, standardisation–divergence), not nearby nouns.
  • ⭐ Extol (praise) vs exonerate (clear of blame) vs exculpate; atonement vs castigation (punishment).
